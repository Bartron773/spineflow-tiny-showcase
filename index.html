About this edition

SpineFlow Tiny

An experimental architecture and spatial-storytelling gallery by Bart Salazar / Simple Minds Studios. These five studies are speculative design work, not verified engineering, measured acoustic specifications or authoritative cultural reconstructions.

The heritage study references regional history as an area for future source review; no endorsement or validation by the Odawa people or tribal governments is implied. The J.O.E. concept is fictional and no live chatbot is active.

This static-first edition does not transmit user input, contact generative-AI providers, use analytics or require API credentials.
